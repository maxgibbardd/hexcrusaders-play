HexCrusaders

A 3D hex-grid strategy board game — hot-seat for 2 to 4 players.

How to play
  • Opening phase: place two settlements and two roads each, snake order.
  • Then roll the dice each turn to collect resources from your junctions.
  • Hover the board to see legal spots — the glow matches the active player.
  • Build roads, settlements and cities. Trade at the bank, at ports, or with each other.
  • First to 10 victory points wins. Roll a 7 and the robber moves.
